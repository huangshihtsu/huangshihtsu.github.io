Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

TL;DR — SUBPOCALYPSE runs on your device. Your subscription data, your kills, and your savings receipts never leave your phone. We can't see them, sell them, or leak them — because we never have them.

1. What the app collects

Nothing. The SUBPOCALYPSE mobile app stores all data (your subscription list, execution history, badges, and settings) locally on your device using your operating system's standard app storage. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. What the app does NOT do

  • No account or sign-up required
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers
  • No access to your email, bank, or contacts
  • No location, microphone, or camera access

3. Website: waitlist & feedback (optional)

Our website offers two voluntary forms. If you choose to use them, we receive:

  • Waitlist — your email address, used only to notify you about launch and major updates. Never sold or shared.
  • Beta feedback form — your rating, device model, message, and optionally your email, used only to fix bugs and improve the app.

Both are stored in Supabase (our database provider, hosted in the US). You can request deletion of your submission at any time via the contact below.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — the website loads fonts from Google; your browser shares standard connection data (like IP address) with Google to fetch them. The mobile app bundles its own rendering and does not phone home.
  • Supabase — stores the voluntary form submissions described in Section 3.
  • Google Play — if you download the app, Google processes your purchase/install data under its own privacy policy.

5. Children

SUBPOCALYPSE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from anyone — children included.

6. Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional Gmail scan feature), we will update this page and bump the date above before the feature ships. Continued use after an update means you accept the new policy.

7. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: privacy@subpocalypse.app
